# FAQ

Short answers to the questions that come up most. None of this requires coding.

**Is an API key the same as my password?** No. Your password logs you into the provider's site. A key is a separate token that tools use on your behalf in the background. You can have several keys and cancel any of them without changing your password.

**What happens if someone gets my key?** They could use the provider as you, on your bill, until you cancel the key. That is why you keep it private and why a spending cap matters. If a key is exposed, cancel it and make a new one right away.

**Can I reuse one key for several tools?** You can, but you should not. One key per tool keeps a single problem from affecting everything. Separate keys are easy to make and easy to cancel individually.

**Will BYOK definitely save me money?** Often, but not always. It saves money when your usage is enough that provider pricing beats a bundled price. For light or occasional use, a bundled subscription may be simpler and cheaper once you count the setup.

**How do I avoid a surprise bill?** Set a hard spending limit at the provider before you use the key much, and turn on usage alerts. With a cap in place, usage stops at your limit instead of climbing.

**Where should I keep my key?** In a password manager, labeled with the provider and the tool it is for. Not in a notes app, not in an email, not in a shared or public document.

**I lost my key. What now?** No problem. Go to the provider, cancel the key you lost, create a new one, and paste it into your tool. Keys are meant to be replaceable.

**Do I need a payment method to get a key?** Usually yes. Most providers require a payment method on file before they issue a usable key, which is also why setting a spending cap first is wise.

**Can a tool see my whole provider account through the key?** A key gives a tool the access that key is scoped for, on that one provider. It does not hand over other services or your login. Using scoped keys where offered narrows this further.
